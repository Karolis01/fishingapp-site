Overview

FishingAPP is designed to work without user accounts, advertising, analytics or behavioural tracking. The developer does not operate a backend that stores your FishingAPP profile, catch history, photos, videos or voice recordings.

Information stored on your device

FishingAPP may store information you choose to create in the app, such as catch records, notes, fishing conditions, locations and related app settings. This information remains on your device unless you deliberately use an iOS feature to save, share, export or back it up.

Photos, video and camera access

FishingAPP can use the camera to photograph catches, support fish measurement and record Action Camera clips or photos. When you ask the app to save media to your Photos library, iOS provides that access. Imported photos are accessed only when you choose them.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone can be used when recording Action Camera clips and when voice commands are enabled. FishingAPP requests on-device speech recognition for supported voice commands. The developer does not receive or store your recordings or recognised speech.

Location

If you grant location permission, FishingAPP can use your position to show relevant weather, tides, map information and fishing conditions, and to associate a location with a catch.

For weather and tide requests, the app may send coordinates rounded to approximately four decimal places to the relevant public data service so that it can return information for that location. The developer does not receive those requests through a FishingAPP-owned server.

Third-party services used for live data

FishingAPP may communicate directly with these services when a related feature is used:

  • MET Norway — weather and marine forecast data.
  • Kartverket — tide/water-level data and map content.
  • Apple MapKit and iOS services — maps and system functionality provided by Apple.

These providers may receive ordinary network information needed to answer a request, such as an IP address and the requested location or map area. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies and applicable law.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

FishingAPP does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. The developer does not sell personal information.

Cookies and this website

This support website is a static GitHub Pages site. FishingAPP does not add analytics, advertising cookies or tracking scripts to it. GitHub operates the hosting infrastructure and may process ordinary server request information according to GitHub's own policies.

Children

FishingAPP is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the developer does not operate user accounts or a FishingAPP backend, the app does not knowingly maintain profiles of children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when FishingAPP features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.
