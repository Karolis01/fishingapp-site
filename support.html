Permissions

Some FishingAPP features need iPhone permissions for the camera, microphone, Photos or location. You can review them in Settings → Apps → FishingAPP.

Weather, tides and maps

Live conditions require an internet connection and, for location-based results, location permission. Availability can also depend on the underlying public data provider for the selected location and time.

Camera and Action Camera

Camera features require camera permission. Action Camera clips with sound also require microphone permission. If you choose to save media to Photos, FishingAPP needs permission to add it to your photo library.

Imported historical catches

FishingAPP can use the date and location available in a photo you select. Historical weather is not currently provided for imported catches. Where supported, historical tide information and on-device moon/daylight calculations may still be available.

Privacy

FishingAPP has no user account, advertising or behavioural analytics. For full details, read the Privacy Policy.
